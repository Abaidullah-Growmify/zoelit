# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Online shoppers evaluating and buying a small curated catalog of everyday premium products across apparel, accessories, home, tech, footwear, and bags.

## Product Purpose

ZoeLit Commerce is an e-commerce storefront for browsing products, viewing detail pages, adding items to a persistent cart, checking out, and managing account/order information.

## Positioning

The store should make a compact catalog feel trustworthy, easy to scan, and ready to buy without inventing unsupported claims about scale, awards, customers, or discounts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities include product listing and detail pages, product categories, cart drawer, checkout, account dashboard, order history, address management, admin management screens, FAQ, contact, and privacy pages. Product facts should come from `lib/data.js` unless the user provides new catalog content.

## Brand Commitments

The public brand name is ZoeLit. Current implementation uses a clean direct voice and must preserve functional commerce clarity.

## Evidence on Hand

Product catalog data lives in `lib/data.js`. Product imagery currently uses configured remote Unsplash URLs. There are no confirmed testimonials, customer logos, awards, sale percentages, or performance benchmarks, so future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

Prioritize product discovery over generic platform claims. Keep purchase actions visible and honest. Make trust cues concrete and tied to existing features. Preserve accessibility, responsive behavior, and fast navigation.
